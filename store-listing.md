## Full description (≤4,000 characters)

> Spending Tracker is a personal finance app for tracking money across
> every account you have — bank accounts, credit cards, cash, savings,
> and investments — in one place, entirely on your own device.
>
> **Your data stays yours.** There's no login, no account, and no
> company server holding your financial information. Everything is
> stored locally on your phone and works fully offline — open the app
> anywhere, no internet required to log a transaction or check a
> balance.
>
> **Track everything that matters:**
> • Multiple accounts in multiple currencies, each with its own running
>   balance
> • Income, expenses, and transfers between your own accounts
> • Recurring transactions — set up a bill or paycheck once, and it
>   keeps logging itself on schedule
> • Categories and tags to see where your money actually goes, with
>   optional monthly budgets per category
> • Net worth tracking with a running trend chart, plus savings goals
>   with pace projections toward each one
> • A "Commitments" view showing everything you're locked into every
>   month, normalized to a single figure regardless of billing cycle
> • Credit card tracking with utilization and a debt payoff projection
> • Export your full transaction history to CSV anytime
>
> **Built for how you actually check your finances** — a calendar view
> of daily spending, month-by-month navigation everywhere, and a
> dashboard that puts your net worth, budgets, and recent activity in
> one glance.
>
> **Global from day one.** Pick your base currency at setup — not
> locked to any one country — and hold accounts in other currencies
> with automatic conversion.
>
> No ads. No tracking. No subscription for the core app — a one-time
> purchase, yours to keep.
