# Claude Design prompt — Erebor 4.x Play Store screenshots

Attach the eight PNGs named below (from this folder), then paste everything under the line.

---

Create a set of 8 Google Play Store phone screenshots for **Erebor Wealth Management**, a private, offline-first personal finance app for Android. Version 4 ("Calm & Clear") is a calm, premium redesign: lots of whitespace, one blue accent, no gradients or glow.

**Canvas and format**
- Each frame is 1080 × 1920 px, portrait (9:16), exported as PNG, one file per frame, named `play-01.png` … `play-08.png`.
- Each frame has a short headline at the top (max 6 words), an optional one-line subhead (max 10 words), and the attached screenshot below it, placed inside a simple modern Android phone frame (thin bezel, rounded corners, no brand logos on the device).
- The phone sits slightly lower than centre, so it can bleed off the bottom edge by up to 10%. Keep headline text clear of Play's corner overlays: 64 px safe margin all round.

**Visual style**
- Background: soft neutral `#F4F4F5` for light frames. For the dark frame (frame 8), use near-black `#050505`.
- Accent: Octet blue `#0D51FB`, used sparingly — a word in the headline or a small shape. Text colour: `#1F1F1F` on light and `#FFFFFF` on dark. Subhead: `#656567`.
- Typography: a clean geometric sans (e.g. Manrope or Inter). Headline bold, about 64 px. Subhead regular, about 34 px. Left-aligned.
- Restrained and premium, like a private bank's app, not a fintech ad. No confetti, coins, 3D money, stock-photo people, emoji, gradients, glows or drop-shadow-heavy effects.
- Keep all eight frames consistent: the same layout grid, type sizes and phone position.

**Rules — important**
- Use the attached screenshots **exactly as they are**. Do not redraw, retype, re-colour, crop out or "improve" anything inside them, and do not invent or change any number, name or label.
- Do not add any person's name, email, phone number or account details anywhere. The sample user in the screenshots is the fictional "Alex"; keep it that way.
- No claims you can't back up: no "#1", "best", "award-winning", ratings, stars, reviews, prices, "free trial" or bank or partner logos.
- English, sentence case, no full stop at the end of headlines.

**The eight frames**

1. **`02-dashboard.png`**
   - Headline: *Your money, at a glance*
   - Subhead: *Net worth, this month and what needs attention*
   - Why: the home screen answers "where do I stand?" — net worth and its change this month, income vs spending against the same days of last month, funds, and anything needing attention.

2. **`03-net-worth.png`**
   - Headline: *See what you're worth*
   - Subhead: *Assets, debt and money set aside, explained*
   - Why: net worth broken into assets, credit card debt, earmarked funds and unallocated money, plus how the assets are split.

3. **`04-analytics.png`**
   - Headline: *Spending, in plain words*
   - Subhead: *Compared with the same days last month*
   - Why: Analytics leads with a sentence ("So far, 33% less than the first 8 days of September"), a running-total chart against last month, and where the money went.

4. **`06-transactions.png`**
   - Headline: *Every rupee, in its place*
   - Subhead: *Grouped by day, with search and filters*
   - Why: transactions grouped by date, with category, account and recurring markers; search and a filter sheet in the header.

5. **`08-funds.png`**
   - Headline: *Set money aside for what's next*
   - Subhead: *Funds earmark savings without moving money*
   - Why: Funds track goals such as a laptop, a trip or an emergency fund, with progress and what's left to go.

6. **`07-accounts.png`**
   - Headline: *All your accounts, one place*
   - Subhead: *Bank, cash, cards, deposits and investments*
   - Why: accounts grouped by type, with balances for the month.

7. **`11-saved.png`**
   - Headline: *Add in seconds*
   - Subhead: *Confirmed instantly, with shortcuts to where it went*
   - Why: after adding a transaction, a banner confirms it and links to the account or the transactions list.

8. **`12-dashboard-dark.png`** — dark frame
   - Headline: *Calm, day or night*
   - Subhead: *Private, offline-first, no account needed*
   - Why: the same Dashboard in dark mode. The subhead states the app's privacy promise: data stays on the phone, with optional Dropbox backup.

**Deliver** the eight PNGs, plus one contact sheet showing all eight side by side so the set can be checked for consistency.
